India Insignia

The National Insignia

National Emblem

  • Lion Capital of Ashoka, Sarnath (MP).

  • Adopted as the state emblem of India: 26 Jan 1950, the day when India became a Republic.

  • Satyameva Jayate from Mundaka Upanishad. It is national motto.

Insignia = चिह्न अधिचिह्न

The Ashoka Pillar

  • Capital means top of a pillar, around 7 feet tall on a 50-40 feet tall pillar, at Sarnath.

  • Sarnath (MP) where the Buddha gave his first sermon.

  • Ashoka, an emperor of Maurya Empire placed pillars like this one at several Buddhist monasteries.

Information from UC Berkeley & image from Cultural Samvad

The Capital

  1. The entire piece was the capital.

  2. Horizontal disk is called abacus, it sits between the capital and the pillar.

  3. Dharmachakra (Wheel of Law), in Buddhism dharma means the universal law that governs the universe.

  4. Four lions on the capital, four cardinal direction, also Buddha, also Ashoka.

  5. The abacus has 4 wheels on it, also representing the dharmachakra.

  6. In between the wheels are four animals: an elephant, a bull, a horse, and a lion. These animals were all associated with Buddhism.

  7. The bottom of the capital is a lotus.

Information and image from UC Berkeley

The National Flag 🇮🇳 (The Tricolor)

  • Saffron (Kesari): representing courage and sacrifice.

  • White: peace and truth.

  • Green: faith and chivalry

  • Chakra of navy blue

  • Design adopted on 22 July 1947

  • Flag Code of India 2002 regulates flat’s use and display.

  • Prevention of Improper Use Act, 1950 & the Prevention of Insults to National Honour Act, 1971

National River

River Ganga (Ganges)

  • The longest in India, flowing over 2,510 kms.

  • Source of Ganga is Gaumukh in Gangotri Himalayan glacier.

  • the Bhagirathi, the Alaknanda, the Mandakini, the Dhauliganga, and the Pindar form Ganga

National Flower

Lotus

  • Scientific Name: Nelumbo Nucifera Gaertn

  • Significance: Purity, beauty, and spirituality

  • Cultural Importance: Sacred in Indian religions like Hinduism and Buddhism

  • Habitat: Grows in wetlands, ponds, and lakes

National Tree

Indian Banyan Tree

  • Scientific name: Ficus Bengalensis

  • It has branches that shoots aerial roots letting it spread like new trees over a large area.

  • This tree is considered immortal due to the characteristic of spreading the whole area with its roots, expanding trunk and its longevity.

National Animal

Royal Bengal Tiger

  • Scientific name: Panthera tigris tigris

  • In 2022, Bengal tiger population was around 3,167-3,682 in India.

  • ‘Project Tiger’ was launched in April 1973.

Image source: Wikipedia

National Fruit

Mango

  • Scientific name: Mangifera indica

  • Around 100 varieties of mangoes in India

National Bird

Indian Peacock

  • Scientific name: Pavo cristatus

National Heritage Animal

Asiatic elephants

National Currency


  • The symbol is a combination of both Devanagiri letter ‘Ra’ and Roman letter ‘R’ with a stripe cutting at the middle to represent the tricolor.

  • The use of Shiro Rekha in Devanagri script is unique to India where letters hangs from the top.

  • The sign designed by D. Udaya Kumar, IIT Guwahati, Assam

Image source: Wikipedia

National Monument

India Gate

  • The war memorial, erected in memory of 90,000 soldiers who lost their lives fighting in the World War I and Afghan War for the British Army.

  • Designed by Sir Edwin Lutyens.

  • Earlier name was All India War Memorial.

National Aquatic Animal

River Dolphin

  • Scientific name: Platanista gangetical

  • It is a critically endangered species in India

  • It is put in Schedule 1 of the Wildlife Protection Act, 1972

National Reptile

King Cobra

  • Scientific name: Ophiophagus hannah

  • It grows up to 3 meters.

National Calendar

  • At the time of independence, the Government of India followed the Gregorian calender based upon the Christian era.

Indian Standard Time (IST)

+5.5 hours of GMT

  • India has only one standard time.

  • Location of Mirzapur (near Allahabad) and the 82°30’E longitude that is used as the reference longitude for IST
